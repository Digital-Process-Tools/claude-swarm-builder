- Doctor rule R03 now checks that every handback state declared on a
  non-terminal edge is referenced by some other edge's `when` as
  `<to>.handback == <state>`, instead of always answering `could-not-check`
  (#3). This surfaces a real, previously-hidden gap in `examples/claude-oss.swarm.json`:
  `run.07`'s `paused` and `blocked` handback states are declared but routed
  nowhere, and the edge is not marked `terminal`. The example ships with this
  finding visible rather than papered over, pending a maintainer decision on
  whether to add `terminal: true` or a routing edge.
