- `/swarm-builder:design` -- an interview command for starting from nothing: one question at a
  time in the user's own domain, a restatement to correct before anything is written, then a
  `swarm.json` proposal (structure only, agents named from the user's vocabulary), rendered via
  `/swarm-builder:read` and looped until the picture is right. Never starts from
  `examples/claude-oss.swarm.json` or any preset, and never guesses `cost_tokens` or any field
  the interview did not settle -- leaves it absent instead. Hands off to `/swarm-builder:compile`
  once the picture is confirmed (#30).
