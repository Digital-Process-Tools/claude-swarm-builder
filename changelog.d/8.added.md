- `scripts/swarm_doctor.py` implements R08: measures the longest spawn chain from each
  flow's root and compares it against `depth_cap` in `.swarm-builder.json`. A cap that is
  not configured is reported as `could-not-check: cap not configured — measure it` rather
  than assumed to be 3; a chain that cycles back on itself is reported the same way rather
  than measured as whatever depth the walk happened to stop at (#8).
