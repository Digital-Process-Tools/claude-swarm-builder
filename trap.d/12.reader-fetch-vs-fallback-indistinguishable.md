Self-review on #12 (reader/reader.html fetch-with-fallback) turned up a non-blocking gap:
once the page tries `fetch('swarm.json')` and falls back to the embedded payload on any
failure, the two states render identically -- no console line, no title/badge, nothing a
viewer can inspect to tell "you are looking at live data served next to this page" from
"the fetch failed (404, network error, bad JSON) and you are looking at whatever was
embedded the last time someone ran scripts/swarm_reader.py, possibly stale."

Ranked against findings.md's table: `misreports` -- can ship behind a trap.d fragment, not
a blocker, not worth its own issue given the low stakes (a static reader page for humans
already looking at the swarm.json/repo). If this bites someone in practice -- confusion
about which flow view is "real" -- the fix is small: a `console.info`/`console.warn` in
the two branches of the fetch chain (`reader/reader.html`'s `.then(init)` / `.catch(() =>
init(embedded))`), or a small on-page indicator. Left for a curation pass to decide whether
it is worth a rule or an issue; not acted on here.
